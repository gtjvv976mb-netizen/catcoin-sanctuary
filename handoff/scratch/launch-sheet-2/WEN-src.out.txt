 Wendy&#039;s Frosty Frights Are Back with 12 New Collectible Toys | All Hallows Geek Home Movies Television Streaming Reviews Toys &amp; Collectibles Games Events &amp; Attractions About Contact Food &amp; Drink , Toys &amp; Collectibles Wendy&#8217;s Frosty Frights Are Back with 12 New Collectible Toys Mike Wilton September 2, 2025, 9:17 am September 2, 2025 (Image: Wendy's) Back in July, I shared that Wendy&#8217;s was teasing the return of its Frosty Frights Kids Meals toys for 2025. On Tuesday, the Frosty Frights made their official return with 12 all-new toys featuring the iconic Wendy&#8217;s treat as a monstrous creation! Like last year when they made their debut , the Frosty Frights will have a series of six core characters, each with a spooky variant. This year&#8217;s lineup of creepy-cute characters includes: Ice Patch &#8211; The King of the Pumpkins. Chilly Gilly &#8211; A Frosty Frights take on the Gil Man. The Yummy &#8211; A Frosty mummy. Captain Coldsnap &#8211; A peg-legged pirate captain. Meltin &#8211; A gooey Frosty Fright. Purrma Frost &#8211; The coolest kitty on the block. In addition to the Frosty Frights toys, Wendy&#8217;s is also offering a new book for children aged three and under, which tells the story of Junior&#8217;s Halloween adoption. The book is called &#8220;A Frosty Halloween,&#8221; and to obtain it, you must select the &#8220;Under 3 Toy Option.&#8221; (Image: Wendy&#8217;s) The official dates for this year&#8217;s promotional period were not readily available at the time of this writing. However, according to the Wendy&#8217;s website , Frosty Frights should now be available at Wendy&#8217;s restaurants.
