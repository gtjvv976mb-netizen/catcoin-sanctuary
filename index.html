Every cat here is a memecoin, or a plan for one. A memecoin has no intrinsic value and no expectation of profit. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

A stock cat is not launched yet until the sanctuary's keeper launches it on StonkFun from the sanctuary's own wallet and the hourly check finds that launch on Solana. Until then it is not a token, and there is nothing to buy. Buy links appear on a card only after that.

A planned cat's ticker is only a plan. Any token under that name that you find before the cat launches is not this cat: only the mint shown on its card after launch is.

The famous cat coins (the badge "Famous coin") already exist and were made by others. Catcoin Sanctuary is not affiliated with any of them, their teams or the companies they are themed on; their cards show their real token, their pair, their market figures with the time they were read, and warnings where there is reason for one. They are all Solana coins; their buy link is their GMGN page.

Catcoin Sanctuary is not affiliated with StonkFun, with the issuers of the tokenised stocks, or with any company whose stock a cat is paired with. A company's own cat or mascot may be described on a card, with its source, but it is never a coin's picture or name.

Every fact, figure and link on a card comes from a source that was opened and dated. Where there is none, the card says "Not measured".

Catcoin Sanctuary is not an animal charity. No real cats live here, and it does not take donations.
