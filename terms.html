Takedowns and contact

If something here is yours and you want it changed or removed, send a direct message to @catcosanctuary on X. That includes:

  • a photo of your cat, or a post of yours we link to;
  • your cat itself: its card, its picture or its name;
  • a name, character, logo or other work you own the rights to.

Tell us the cat's name or link and what you would like removed. We take things down promptly when the request comes from the owner or the rights holder, and we may ask you to show that it is yours. Coins that are already on a blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone, but we can stop showing or promoting them.
